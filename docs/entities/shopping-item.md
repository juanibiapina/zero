# Shopping item

## What it is

A Shopping item is one thing you need to buy, on your Shopping list: "Milk",
"Batteries for the smoke alarm". It has only its text. You tick it off when you
buy it and it leaves the list. There is one list, and it never closes or
starts over: items stay until you buy them.

## Why it is its own block

Things to buy used to go on Home as Tasks, where they crowded out the work.
Shopping items never reach Home, Upcoming or Projects, and the list is the
place to build shopping features that Tasks will not share.

## Rules

- A new item goes to the bottom of the list.
- Buying an item hides it, with Undo, which puts it back in its place. Bought
  items are kept for later tracking; nothing shows them yet.
- Holding and dragging an item reorders the list.
- Tapping an item opens the same sheet as a Task: the check circle and the
  text. Its text is literal: "milk tomorrow" sets no date.
- An item cannot be deleted, like a Task. Buying it is how it leaves.

## How it looks

Exactly like a Task row: a check circle and the text, with no icon, date or
swipe. On mobile the list is under Browse, as Shopping list; on the web it is
Shopping in the navigation. Home's add drawer, and the add form on the web,
have a Shopping option that adds an item and shows "Added to shopping list"
with View, which opens the list.

## Interactions

- Task, Project, Waiting, After, Medicine, Night routine, Recording: none, on
  purpose.
- Agents: list, add, edit, move, buy, and put a bought item back.

## Left out on purpose

- Delete.
- Quantities, stores, categories, and suggested or reusable items.
- Closing a list, starting a new one, or dates.
- Shopping items in Projects.

## Ideas

- Show what you bought and when.
