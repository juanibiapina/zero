# Night routine

## What it is

The Night routine is the checklist you go through before bed: "Wechseldusche on
legs", "Meditate". There is one, built in. You tick off its steps in the
evening and press Going to bed when you put the phone away. What you did not do
stays undone, and the next evening the list starts fresh.

## Why it is its own block

A habit you skip on some nights is not work. As a repeating Task it would sit
on Home every day and pile up as overdue. The Night routine never reaches the
Task list, forgives a skipped night, and still records which nights you did each
step.

## Rules

- A night takes the date of its evening. From 08:00 to 23:59 the current night
  is today's; from 00:00 to 07:59 it is yesterday's, so a step done at 00:30
  counts for the evening before.
- Each night is its own page: its steps, the steps done, the opening time and
  the bedtime. Tonight's page starts as a copy of the latest earlier page, with
  nothing done. Changing the steps or the opening time changes tonight and the
  nights after it; earlier nights keep the list they had.
- Completing a step hides it, with Undo. Steps keep their order and come back
  open the next night.
- Going to bed ends the night and records the time, with Undo. A night you do
  not end closes at 08:00 without a bedtime. A closed night takes no more
  changes.
- The 🌙 button shows on Home from 21:00, the opening time. The page has no
  control to change it yet. The routine itself is always open from Browse, and
  a step done before 21:00 counts for that night.
- Every night is kept with the steps done and the bedtime, for a History view
  later. The page does not show it yet.

## How it looks

A 🌙 button in Home's header, from the opening time until the night closes,
when the routine has steps. It opens the Night routine page, which Browse also
lists. The page shows the open steps as rows with a circle and a collapsed "Done tonight (N)" with the times. A round bed button
left of the + is Going to bed. The + button adds a step. Tapping a step renames or removes it;
holding and dragging it reorders it. After Going to bed the page shows "Good
night · 1 of 2 done · bed at 23:42" and the steps left undone. Back returns to
where you opened it: Home from the 🌙 button, Browse from Browse.

## Interactions

- Task, Project, Waiting, After, Medicine: none, on purpose. Steps are not
  Tasks and never appear on Home, Upcoming or Projects.
- Agents: none.

## Left out on purpose

- Other routines, such as a Morning routine.
- Reminders.
- Editing a past night.
- The Night routine on the web.
- Two devices editing the list of steps offline on the same night: the later
  list wins. Steps done on two devices both count.
